Cases

Only closed cases enter this set: the rule text already existed, execution was delayed or conditioned, the delay ended for reasons other than the firm's own misconduct, and the landing point is observable. A case failing any of those four conditions is not construction material; it belongs on the open side, not here.

The set grows one closed case at a time. It is not filled to a fixed number. An item that is still open — text exists but the outcome isn't yet observable — lives on the watchlist until it clears all four conditions.
