STAGE 01

Object of the rule

What the text names as its target. Read from the article itself, before anything downstream is considered.

TypeTriggerExample
Attribute-based A continuous value measured on the item itself: content share, ownership percentage, technical lineage. 0.1% rare-earth content; 50% ownership; U.S.-origin technology share of a foreign-made chip.
Relational A position in a network: nationality, headquarters location, a contract chain. Headquarters in Country Group D:5; a re-export clause running through a third-country buyer.
Conduct-based A future behavior the text conditions on, not a present property. A disciplinary commitment made as a condition of a suspended denial order.
