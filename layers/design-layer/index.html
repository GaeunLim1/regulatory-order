LAYER 02

Design Layer

Not whether the rule existed before the case. Whether this rulemaking sits inside the article's delegated authority, or extends that boundary, and separately, what fires it.

Axis 1: scope of authority

TypeReading
Scope-consistentThe rulemaking moves inside the range already delegated to the article. Lower legal exposure once in force.
Boundary-extendingThe rulemaking extends the article's own reach: a new jurisdictional or definitional line, not a new number inside an old one.

How the axis 1 reading is made

This is not a legal ruling. No court has to weigh in for this reading to exist, and it is made before any outcome is observable. It rests on comparing three things, read from the text itself:

StepReading
Delegating clauseThe statute or article this rulemaking draws its authority from, cited directly. What scope does it hand over in explicit terms?
Stated purposeWhat the rulemaking body itself says it is doing: preamble, stated rationale, legislative history.
Operative text scopeWhat the rule's own operative text actually reaches, read on its own, independent of what it says it is doing.
DeltaThe gap between the first two and the third. No gap: scope-consistent. A gap that draws a new jurisdictional or definitional line: boundary-extending.

Filled per rulemaking, on the item page, alongside the axis 1 conclusion, so the reading can be checked against the text rather than taken on the conclusion alone.

Axis 2: trigger mechanism

TypeReading
Numeric triggerFires on a threshold or a calendar date, independent of any actor's behavior.
Conduct triggerFires on a breach or a failure to perform a condition. Creates a latent interval between the breach and its detection that a numeric trigger cannot produce.

The two axes, crossed

Scope-consistentBoundary-extending
Numeric triggerThreshold adjusted inside existing scope.New threshold extends jurisdiction itself.
Conduct triggerCompliance condition added inside existing scope.New conduct standard extends jurisdiction itself.
