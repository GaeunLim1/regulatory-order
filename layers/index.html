Master Frame

Stage 1 and 2 read a single rule: what it targets, and whether it sits inside or redraws the agency's authority. Stage 3 reads more than one rule together, when more than one applies to the same firm. Stage 4 turns the reading into a firm-level exposure matrix, informed by Stage 3.
