Master Frame

Master Frame

Stage 1 and 2 read a single rule: what it targets, and whether it sits inside or redraws the agency's authority. Stage 3 reads more than one rule together, when more than one applies to the same firm. Stage 4 turns the reading into a firm-level exposure matrix, informed by Stage 3.

Stage What When How
01 · Object of the Rule What the text names as its target: an attribute (a threshold, a content share), a relation (a nationality, a contract chain), or a condition (a compliance behavior). when this object is named in the text Read from the official text of the article or rule.
02 · Design Layer Whether this rulemaking sits inside the agency's authorizing text, or redraws that line, and whether it fires on a threshold or on an actor's conduct. The scope reading is traced against the authorizing text, the stated aim, and the reach of the operative text, not asserted as a conclusion. at the rulemaking's confirmation Checked against the authorizing statute for scope and trigger type.
03 · Cross-Reading Runs when more than one rule applies to the same firm: object comparison, design comparison, and a reading of jurisdiction and licensing authority across both regulators. when a second rule applies Reads the two Stage 1 and 2 outputs together, not each in isolation.
04 · Cost Layer A matrix, not a label. For each firm inside the object's reach: distance from the named target, revenue share exposed, substitution available, prior positioning. Informed by Stage 3, when it ran. any point before the outcome is known Filled firm by firm; no outcome required to complete it.
