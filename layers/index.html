Master frame

Layers

This is not a legal validity check. It traces a rule from the design intent behind it through firm-level exposure to the point a company actually has to comply, independent of whether implementation gets delayed or staged along the way. Two layers carry fixed types. Object of the rule and Convergence classify: a case falls into one named type or another. Design Layer crosses two independent axes and traces the reading, not just the conclusion. Cost Layer carries no types; it is a matrix of firm-level variables, filled before any outcome is known.
