LAYER 04

Convergence

Reads the Cost Layer matrix and classifies. Not a narration of what happened, but a rule applied to the variables already on the matrix.

The test

Two questions, asked in order against the Cost Layer matrix.

QuestionReading
1. Scale proportional?Is the size of the outcome proportional to the size of the trigger, or disproportionate to it?
2. Landing point match?If proportional, does the outcome land on the named target, or on a different actor in the matrix?

Four types

TypeCondition
Direct hitProportional, lands on the named target.
ReallocationProportional, lands on a different actor in the matrix.
AmplificationDisproportionate. Outcome scale exceeds the trigger's designed scope.
DiffusionNever lands on one actor; absorbed across the matrix as a new baseline.

Two further states can appear under a conduct trigger only (see Design Layer): a latent interval before a breach is detected, and a conditional reinstatement that returns a firm to operation under permanent oversight rather than closing the case.
