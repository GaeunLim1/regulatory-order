APPLIED EXAMPLE

Cross-Reading, Applied to Firm X

Stage 1 and 2 are read per rule. This page runs only when more than one rule applies to the same firm. It does not re-test each rule. It looks for the connection point that remains after each department has already cleared its own object. Read through here against Firm X, the firm introduced in Section D of the Applied Example.

0. Gate

If none of these is yes, stop. Stage 3 does not run.

#QuestionReading
0.1Does the BIS Affiliates Rule attach, or could it attach, to this firm or to a party in this transaction.Yes. Firm X's joint-venture partner is 50 percent or more owned by an Entity-Listed party.
0.2Do China rare earth controls attach, or could they attach: mark which: Announcement 18 (already in force), 61 (extraterritorial content / affiliate), 62 (technology).Yes, Announcement 61, content branch. Firm X's magnets carry roughly 2 to 4 percent Chinese-origin heavy rare earths, above the 0.1 percent threshold. No fact places Firm X under 62 or 18.
0.3Do both axes touch the same legal entity, plant, product line, or transfer.Yes. Both facts sit on the same magnet product line.

0.1 through 0.3 are yes. Stage 3 runs.

#QuestionReading
A2.1Does this matter carry more than one object type (relational × attribute, or relational × conduct).Yes: relational (BIS) × attribute (Announcement 61, content).
A2.2Does the primary data for each type sit in a different team or system.Yes: ownership data with legal / trade, content-share data with procurement / origin.
A2.3Has each team already recorded “not covered” or “no license required” on its own object.
A2.4After those clearances, does one of the connection points below remain.Yes: the JV partner is both the Affiliates party and the plant producing the content-bearing magnets.

Connection points. One yes is enough to treat the matter as a cross-read object:

A listed affiliate receives rare-earth process technology, line support, or remote maintenance.
A listed affiliate makes or receives an item that carries Chinese-origin rare-earth content or Chinese-origin technology.
A third-country to third-country movement carries both U.S. content or FDP and Chinese content or technology on the same item.
The same foreign plant is an Affiliates party and a recipient of Announcement 62 technology.

D. Inside the firm

Object comparison predicts the split: a person-type object routes to legal; a thing- or technology-type object routes to the plant. If this section is empty, A through C can be filled and the cross-read still disappears at implementation.

#QuestionY / N / unknown
D1.1Do ownership screening, bill of materials or content share, and process or technology-transfer documents attach to the same internal paper under one transaction ID.
D1.2Are technical support, commissioning, and remote maintenance treated as an export paper, or as ordinary plant work that never reaches legal.
D1.3Is there an approval path that can clear Affiliates without content data.
D1.4Is there an approval path that can clear 62 or content share without ownership data.
D1.5If D1.3 or D1.4 is yes, was that path used on this transaction.
D2.1Does each object type have a different outside advisor.
D2.2Does any advisor’s scope include the cross-read object (party × technology × content).
D2.3Did each opinion stop at “no issue within scope.”
D2.4Did someone inside the firm commission the cross-read, or only the separate objects.

If D1.3 or D1.4 was used on this transaction, independent compliance has already happened. If D2.2 is no, the firm bought Stage 1 and 2 and did not buy Stage 3.

E. Reading

ConditionReading

Stage 3 starts when the object types are named. It ends when a team or advisor has the connection point as its own item, not as a leftover from two clearances.

What this page hands to Stage 4

Whether the cross-read sits on the same product line (revenue share).
Whether substitution is object-specific: a supplier change may clear content or Announcement 18; it does not by itself clear a JV ownership tie.
Which regulator already has a filing or a pending application.
Whether the November lift-or-extend branches change the C5 filing sequence.
