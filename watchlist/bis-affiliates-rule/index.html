Design Layer, scope trace

StepReading
Delegating clauseExport Control Reform Act of 2018 (50 U.S.C. 4801 et seq.), implemented through EAR Part 744. Part 744 authorizes BIS to restrict named parties on the Entity List and Military End-User List. It names the listed party itself as the object of restriction.
Stated purposeThe rule's preamble states the aim is closing an evasion channel: listed parties routing transactions through affiliates that are separately incorporated and so fell outside the listing.
Operative text scopeThe rule's operative text does not restrict the listed party's conduct. It creates a new, freestanding restriction on any entity meeting a 50% ownership test, whether or not that entity has done anything itself.
DeltaThe delegating clause and the preamble both center on the listed party. The operative text reaches a class of entities defined only by an ownership percentage, with no conduct or listing decision of their own. That is a new class of restricted party, not a closer read of the existing one, which is why this rule is read as boundary-extending rather than scope-consistent.
