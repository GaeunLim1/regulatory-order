Watchlist

An item lands here when the rule text already exists but at least one of the four case-library conditions has not yet cleared — usually because the delay hasn't ended, or the landing point isn't observable yet. Object of the Rule, Design Layer, and Cost Layer are filled the same way a case fills them: firm by firm, before any outcome is known. Convergence stays open, and a response plan (owner, actions, review date, escalation trigger) sits alongside it — a closed case doesn't need one; an open item does.

An item moves to the case library once all four conditions are met, and stays here otherwise.
