--- layout: default title: Overview description: "A framework for reading export-control and national-security rules: what the text names, what authority it claims, who carries exposure before the outcome, and where the outcome actually lands." ---

It traces a rule from the intent behind its design through the point a firm actually has to comply, independent of whether implementation is delayed, staged, or stayed along the way.

Master frame

01 Object of the Rule What the text names as its target: an attribute, a relation, or a conduct condition. → 02 Design Layer Whether the rulemaking sits inside the agency's authorizing text or redraws that line, and whether it fires on a threshold or on conduct. → 03 Cross-Reading Runs when more than one rule applies to the same firm: object comparison, design comparison, jurisdiction and licensing authority. → 04 Cost Layer A matrix, not a label: distance from the object, revenue share exposed, substitution available, prior positioning. Informed by Stage 3. →
