--- layout: default title: Overview description: "A framework for reading export-control and national-security rules: what the text names, what authority it claims, who carries exposure before the outcome, and where the outcome actually lands." ---

It traces a rule from the intent behind its design through the point a firm actually has to comply, independent of whether implementation is delayed, staged, or stayed along the way.

Master frame

Layer What When How
Object of the rule What the text names as its target: an attribute (a threshold, a content share), a relation (a nationality, a contract chain), or a condition (a compliance behavior). when this object is named in the text Read from the official text of the article or rule.
Design Layer Whether this rulemaking sits inside the agency's authorizing text, or redraws that line, and whether it fires on a threshold or on an actor's conduct. The scope reading is traced against the authorizing text, the stated aim, and the reach of the operative text, not asserted as a conclusion. at the rulemaking's confirmation Checked against the authorizing statute for scope and trigger type.
Cost Layer A matrix, not a label. For each firm inside the object's reach: distance from the named target, revenue share exposed, substitution available, prior positioning. any point before the outcome is known Filled firm by firm; no outcome required to complete it.
Convergence Reads the Cost Layer matrix and classifies: direct hit, reallocation, amplification, diffusion. after the mismatch surfaces Matched against scale-proportionality and landing-point rules, not narrated case by case.
