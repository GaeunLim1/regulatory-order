EXPORT CONTROL·NATIONAL SECURITY

A reading order for regulation

The frame traces a rule from the intent behind its design to the point a firm actually has to comply, whether or not implementation is delayed, staged, or stayed along the way.

STAGE 01 Object of the Rule What the text names as its target. Read from the article itself, before anything downstream. attribute · relational · conduct → STAGE 02 Design Layer Whether the rule stays inside the agency's authority or redraws the line, and what fires it. scope × trigger → STAGE 03 Cross-Reading Runs only when more than one rule reaches the same firm. Reads them together, not side by side. object · design · jurisdiction → STAGE 04 Cost Layer A per-firm matrix, filled before the outcome is known. It carries no fixed types: a label here would already be a result. variables × scenarios
